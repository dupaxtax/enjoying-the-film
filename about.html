About The Podcast
Chad and Chase are a father and son that love analyzing and discussing movies and pop culture. They love to share music they love, watch movies that linger with them forever, and even argue with friends, family, and each other about what makes a cinematic masterpiece. Here, and on social media, they rank things, respond to things, and look at the pop culture landscape.
